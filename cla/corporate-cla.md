# Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement — DRAFT, NOT YET IN FORCE

**Not reviewed by counsel. Do not present to a contributor.**

Where contributions to the AGPL-3.0-only core are made by employees in the course of their
employment, the employer must grant the same rights set out in `cla/individual-cla.md` — in
particular the sublicensing right the commercial tier depends on.

## Structure — draft

1. The company grants the rights in the Individual CLA, on behalf of itself and its employees,
   for all contributions made by listed employees.
2. **Schedule A** lists authorised employees; the company keeps it current.
3. The company confirms it holds the rights it is granting.
4. Signed by someone authorised to bind the company.

## Open items for counsel

- Whether Schedule A should be maintained by the company or by the project.
- What happens to contributions made by an employee before they appear on Schedule A.
- Governing law.
