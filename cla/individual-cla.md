# Individual Contributor Licence Agreement — DRAFT, NOT YET IN FORCE

**This text has not been reviewed by counsel and must not be presented to a contributor until it
has been.** PRD §20.3 requires a legal review to confirm the exact contributor documents before
public launch.

## Why this exists

Contributions to the AGPL-3.0-only core are dual-licensed. The project offers an AGPL-free
commercial licence for private deployment and OEM use, which is only possible if the project
steward holds relicensing rights over every line in the core. Without that, the commercial tier
cannot be granted (PRD §13.2).

The Apache-2.0 packages — `spec`, `connectors`, `sdk` — do **not** require this agreement. They use
DCO sign-off (see `DCO.txt`). Contribute there without signing anything.

## Grant — draft terms for review

1. **Copyright licence.** You grant the project steward a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
   royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly
   display, publicly perform, sublicense and distribute your contributions and derivative works.
2. **Sublicensing.** That licence expressly includes the right to sublicense under terms of the
   steward's choosing, including proprietary terms. This is the clause the commercial tier depends on.
3. **Patent licence.** You grant a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable
   patent licence covering your contributions, terminable as to any party bringing patent litigation
   alleging that your contribution infringes.
4. **You retain ownership.** You keep full rights to use your contribution for any other purpose.
5. **Representations.** You confirm each contribution is your original work, that you are legally
   entitled to grant this licence, and that if your employer holds rights in your work you have
   permission or a waiver.
6. **No warranty.** Contributions are provided as-is.
